# Build Your Ironman Brain

## The Idea

Most training tools know a workout. Few know the athlete's history, goals, travel,
work, family obligations, injury boundaries, recovery patterns, and clinical
context at the same time.

`ironman-brain` gives an MCP-capable AI a durable local context system. Human-authored
Markdown explains the person. Betterness supplies permissioned, source-attributed
health and wearable data. The agent helps turn both into better questions, reviews,
and handoffs.

## What You Can Do

1. Run a one-question-at-a-time athlete interview.
2. Confirm a durable athlete profile instead of repeating context in every chat.
3. Connect supported wearables and health sources through Betterness.
4. Ask an AI to compare subjective experience with activity, sleep, vitals, body
   composition, biomarkers, and labs when those sources are available.
5. Produce daily decisions, weekly reviews, race retrospectives, and concise notes
   for a coach or clinician.
6. Preserve where every observation came from and what remains uncertain.

## What Makes It Different

- **Context before metrics:** the system learns why and how the person trains.
- **Permissioned live data:** users choose what Betterness may expose through MCP.
- **Lineage before confidence:** each derived note records tools, devices, dates,
  timezone, retrieval time, and gaps.
- **Portable by design:** the vault is plain Markdown and can work with Claude,
  Codex, ChatGPT, OpenClaw, or another client that can read local files and use MCP.
- **Human decisions remain human:** the agent does not diagnose, prescribe, or
  replace a coach or clinician.
