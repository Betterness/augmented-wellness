# Show Notes

## Guest

Eduardo della Maggiora is founder and CEO of Betterfly and an Ironman athlete.

## Conversation

- Chile, New York, J.P. Morgan, and the decision to move to Africa
- the personal experiences that changed Eduardo's definition of success
- the origin and growth of Betterfly
- healthier behavior, financial protection, and social impact
- why company-building and Ironman reward similar forms of resilience
- using Obsidian and local AI agents as a personal context system
- years of training, wearable, biomarker, nutrition, and race history
- personalized health and the n-of-one
- why Eduardo invested in Betterness

## Build With the Episode

[`ironman-brain`](./ironman-brain) is a private-by-default Obsidian starter vault
inspired by the system discussed in the episode. It can be enriched with the
verified public Betterness MCP tools while preserving data lineage and human
decision boundaries.
