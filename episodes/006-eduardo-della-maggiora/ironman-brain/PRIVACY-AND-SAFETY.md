# Privacy and Safety

## Keep Private

Never commit or publish:

- Betterness MCP keys or other credentials;
- raw wearable, lab, or health exports;
- identifiable medical records;
- personal daily notes or generated reports;
- addresses, birth dates, phone numbers, or emergency details;
- a coach or clinician's private communication without permission.

Use a private local copy of this vault for real data. The public GitHub repository
is a template and learning artifact.

## Consent and Control

- Connect only sources the athlete chooses.
- Use only the minimum data needed for the current question.
- Ask before writing confirmed conclusions into the athlete profile.
- Ask before preparing information for another person.
- Preserve the ability to revoke the MCP key and disconnect providers.

## Interpretation Boundaries

- Wearables estimate; they do not observe every relevant variable.
- HRV, sleep stages, calorie burn, VO2 max estimates, and readiness scores can be
  useful while remaining noisy or device-dependent.
- Correlation does not establish causation.
- Laboratory context depends on methods, units, timing, symptoms, and clinical
  history.
- Training response is individual and can change across age, illness, environment,
  equipment, and life load.

## Urgent Concerns

Do not use this vault to delay urgent care. Seek appropriate help for chest pain,
fainting, severe shortness of breath, neurological symptoms, significant bleeding,
suspected heat illness, or another acute concern.
