## Agent Questions

- Which recent sessions are most comparable to race demands?
- Which assumptions about pace, power, fueling, hydration, or heat have actually
  been rehearsed?
- What changed since the original plan?
- Where are travel and life load likely to reduce available recovery?
- What decision needs to be made now, and what can wait?
- What belongs with the coach, physician, dietitian, or race organizer?

## Post-Race Learning

- What happened versus plan?
- Which data are trustworthy?
- What did the athlete perceive before the data confirmed it?
- Which lesson should become a decision rule, and which was race-specific?
