# Weekly Review

## Review Window

- Week ending:
- Timezone:
- Current training block:
- Important life events:

## Questions the Review Must Answer

1. What work was planned, completed, changed, or skipped?
2. What did volume, duration, frequency, intensity proxies, and sport mix do?
3. How did sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, symptoms, soreness, mood, and life load
   move together or disagree?
4. Is there enough comparable data to call this a pattern?
5. What adaptation appears plausible, and what is still speculation?
6. What should continue unchanged?
7. What is the smallest adjustment worth testing next week?
8. What needs coach or clinician review?

## Required Output

- **Week in one sentence**
- **Completed work** with source coverage
- **Recovery context** with conflicts preserved
- **One thing learned**
- **One decision for next week**
- **One question for the athlete**
- **Professional handoff**, if needed
- **Data lineage**: tools, devices, ranges, timezone, retrieval time

Save the result from `templates/WEEKLY-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md` rather than rewriting
this operating note.
