# Prepare a Human Handoff

The agent should make professional conversations more efficient, not compete with
the professional.

## Prompt

> Create a one-page brief for my [coach / physician / physical therapist /
> dietitian]. Use only confirmed profile facts, my notes, and permissioned
> Betterness data. State the review window, timezone, devices, and source gaps.
> Separate measured changes from my symptoms and your inferences. Do not diagnose
> or prescribe. End with the three decisions or questions that need this person's
> expertise.
