# Claims and Language Standard

## Safe Public Claims

- The artifact helps organize personal context and permissioned health data.
- Betterness MCP can expose verified categories described in
  [[../03-data-and-mcp/CAPABILITY-MAP]].
- The system can support better questions, reviews, and professional handoffs.
- An Obsidian vault remains human-readable and portable.

## Claims That Require Qualification

- A trend is meaningful only when source, coverage, context, and comparability are
  adequate.
- A relationship between training, sleep, HRV, nutrition, or symptoms is an
  observation or hypothesis unless stronger evidence exists.
- A biological-age or fitness estimate is a model output, not a complete account
  of health.

## Do Not Claim

- That the agent diagnoses illness, prevents injury, or replaces a professional.
- That a single wearable score determines readiness or training.
- That every new Betterness workout surface is already available through MCP.
- That the system autonomously prescribes medication, supplements, or medical care.
- That Eduardo or Betterness endorses a user's specific training decision.

## Evidence Practice

When adding research:

1. Prefer primary peer-reviewed studies, consensus statements, and authoritative
   professional guidance.
2. Record title, authors, year, DOI or stable URL, population, intervention or
   exposure, outcome, and principal limitations.
3. Distinguish trained athletes from recreational populations and association
   from intervention.
4. Do not upload copyrighted full papers unless redistribution rights are clear.
5. Link evidence to a decision question rather than collecting papers for volume.
