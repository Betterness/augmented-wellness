# Episode 006 - Eduardo della Maggiora

*The founder who treats company-building, endurance, and personal health as one
continuous practice.*

**Private review:** [YouTube (unlisted)](https://youtu.be/HTHKB1OriXw)

Eduardo della Maggiora is the founder and CEO of Betterfly and an Ironman athlete.
In this conversation, he traces the path from investment banking to Africa,
Betterfly, and endurance sport, then opens the local Obsidian and AI system he uses
to learn from years of training, wearable, biomarker, nutrition, and race data.

The episode turns on a practical question: what becomes possible when an AI can
understand the athlete, the life around the training, and the longitudinal data,
instead of reacting to one score at a time?

## The Artifact: `ironman-brain`

Eduardo's local agent inspired **[`ironman-brain`](./ironman-brain)**, an
Obsidian-first starter system for building a private endurance and health context
vault. It combines a one-question-at-a-time athlete interview with permissioned
Betterness MCP data, explicit source lineage, daily and weekly review loops, race
notes, and coach or clinician handoffs.

> What actually ships today: a portable Markdown vault, agent operating contract,
> interview, decision loops, templates, safety rules, and setup for the verified
> public Betterness MCP tools. It does not include Eduardo's private data, make
> medical decisions, or expose product capabilities that are not yet in the live
> MCP catalog.
