# Doctor Visit Prep Questions

Use these with a clinician after collecting labs, DEXA, VO2 max, or wearable trends.

## Before the visit

- What are the three most important goals I want this data to support?
- Which numbers are trends, and which are one-time snapshots?
- Which results need medical interpretation before I act?
- What symptoms, family history, medications, or supplements could change the interpretation?

## Blood work

- Which markers are most important for my age, history, goals, and current symptoms?
- Are any results borderline but worth watching because of my personal context?
- What should be repeated in three to six months?
- What lifestyle change is most likely to move the marker we care about?

## DEXA and body composition

- What matters most here: lean mass, body fat percentage, visceral fat, bone density, or asymmetry?
- Is anything surprising relative to my activity level?
- Should I change training, nutrition, or recovery because of this report?

## VO2 max and performance

- How should I interpret this VO2 max for my age, training status, and testing protocol?
- What would improve it safely: zone 2, intervals, strength, weight change, or consistency?
- When should I retest, and how do we compare results fairly?

## Recovery and habit design

- What is the smallest plan I can repeat for a year?
- What would be too aggressive and likely to fail?
- How should I use HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, or soreness without overreacting to noise?

## Medical boundaries

- Which decisions should I not make from AI or wearable data alone?
- What red flags should prompt a real medical visit?
- What is educational, and what is treatment?
