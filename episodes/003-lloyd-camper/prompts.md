# Bett-i Prompts — Healthspan Baseline

These prompts are designed to make a medical conversation more productive. They are not diagnostic prompts.

## Organize my baseline

```text
I am preparing for a clinician visit. Help me organize my healthspan baseline into: goals, current habits, recent labs, body-composition data, VO2/cardio data, sleep/recovery signals, symptoms, medications/supplements, and questions for my doctor. Do not diagnose me. Flag anything that requires clinician interpretation.
```

## Turn a report into questions

```text
I am going to paste a health report. Summarize it in plain English, separate facts from interpretation, and create a list of questions I should ask my doctor. Do not recommend treatment or diagnose. If a value depends on context, say what context is needed.
```

## Make the plan realistic

```text
Based on the clinician's recommendations I paste next, help me turn them into a weekly plan I can realistically follow. Keep it conservative and sustainable. Include what to track, what to repeat, and when I should check back with the clinician.
```

## Avoid overreacting to wearables

```text
Look at these sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and activity trends. Help me identify patterns and questions, not diagnoses. Tell me what might be noise, what might be worth watching, and what should be discussed with a qualified professional.
```
