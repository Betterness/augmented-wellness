# 06 — Betterness Connection

This portfolio becomes more powerful when it is connected to your real health data.

As Bett-i migrates into [betterness.ai](https://betterness.ai), the intended workflow is:

1. Create your Healthspan Baseline Context Portfolio.
2. Go to [betterness.ai](https://betterness.ai).
3. Create or connect your account.
4. Upload labs or order labs through Betterness when available.
5. Connect wearables and health data sources.
6. Ask Bett-i to organize your baseline and prepare better clinician questions.

## What Bett-i can add

With permission, Bett-i can help connect:

- lab history;
- DEXA and VO2 max reports;
- sleep and recovery trends;
- training and movement data;
- goals and constraints;
- prior questions and clinician notes you choose to provide.

## Better questions once connected

- What does my baseline show across labs, fitness, body composition, and recovery?
- What data is missing before my next clinician visit?
- Which changes are worth discussing with a doctor?
- What habits are realistic enough to maintain?
- What should I track before changing anything?

## Boundary

Bett-i should help organize data, explain terminology, and prepare questions. It should not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a qualified clinician.
