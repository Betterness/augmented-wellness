## Trend questions

- What can be compared to prior results?
- What is new?
- What has changed meaningfully?
- What is old enough that it should be repeated?
- What is missing entirely?

## Interpretation boundaries

Mark each item:

- **Organize only** — the assistant can summarize and format.
- **Explain terminology** — the assistant can explain what the marker generally means.
- **Clinician required** — interpretation depends on medical context.
- **Do not act on this alone** — medication, diagnosis, treatment, or procedure decisions.

## Questions that create insight

- What marker am I over-focused on?
- What marker am I ignoring?
- What report does not mean much without trend history?
- What would a clinician need before giving a serious answer?
- What should Bett-i pull in automatically once connected to my account?
