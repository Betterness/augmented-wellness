## Metabolic questions

- What does my current eating pattern optimize for?
- Am I trying to lose fat, gain muscle, improve energy, improve labs, or all of them?
- Am I eating enough protein for my goals?
- Do I understand how alcohol, sleep, stress, and late meals affect my data?
- What would I like to test before making a bigger change?

## Questions that create insight

- Which habit is most likely to last?
- Which habit sounds impressive but never survives real life?
- What should be made easier instead of more intense?
- What does my doctor need to know about my actual week?
