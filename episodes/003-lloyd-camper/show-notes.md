# Augmented Wellness — Episode 003: Dr. Lloyd Camper

## One-sentence promise

A longevity and sports-medicine physician explains why most people wait for the warning light — and how labs, DEXA, VO2 max, recovery, and realistic habits can become a smarter healthspan baseline.

## Recommended title

Dr. Lloyd Camper: stop waiting for the warning light — build your healthspan baseline

## Short description

Dr. Lloyd Camper joins Augmented Wellness for a practical conversation about sports medicine, longevity, Cenegenics, and the difference between diagnosing illness and promoting health.

We cover exercise as medicine, the gas-tank model of preventive care, why blood work only matters inside medical context, how DEXA and VO2 max reveal different parts of the body's operating system, and where AI can help organize data without replacing a physician.

In the final section, Demian brings his own DEXA and VO2 max reports into Bett-i so Lloyd can react live. The public artifact intentionally does not publish those private reports; it turns the conversation into a safe checklist and question bank anyone can use before a clinician visit.

Nothing in this episode is medical advice. Use this conversation for education and work with a qualified clinician for personal medical decisions.

## In this episode, we discuss

1. Why Lloyd frames exercise prescription as one of the most powerful forms of preventive medicine.
2. The gas-tank model: traditional care often starts when the warning light comes on; longevity tries to keep the tank from drifting empty.
3. Why more access to blood work is valuable, but a physician still needs history, exam, goals, and context.
4. How DEXA and VO2 max can make body composition and cardiorespiratory fitness easier to understand.
5. Where regenerative medicine, PRP, stem cells, cold plunge, and sauna need nuance rather than hype.
6. Why Bett-i is most useful when it turns reports into better questions for a qualified clinician.
