# Claims and Guardrails

## Safe public claims

- Dr. Lloyd Camper discusses a longevity and sports-medicine approach centered on baseline data, exercise, body composition, VO2 max, labs, recovery, and sustainable habits.
- The episode contrasts reactive care with a more proactive healthspan mindset.
- Bett-i is shown as a way to organize health data and prepare better questions for a clinician.
- The episode is educational and does not provide medical advice.

## Avoid saying

- Do not say the episode proves a protocol works for everyone.
- Do not imply AI can diagnose, prescribe, or replace a physician.
- Do not imply DEXA, VO2 max, HRV, or blood work alone determines health.
- Do not promote regenerative medicine, stem cells, peptides, cold plunge, sauna, supplements, or biomarkers as universal treatment.
- Do not publish Demian's private medical reports as public data.

## Better framing

Say: "use data to ask better questions with a qualified clinician."

Do not say: "use data to diagnose yourself."
