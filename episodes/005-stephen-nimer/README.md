# Episode 005: Dr. Stephen Nimer

Dr. Stephen Nimer leads Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center and is a
physician-scientist, cancer researcher, and cyclist. In this episode, he and
Demian discuss what cancer care looks like when the patient is treated as a
partner, how the Dolphins Cancer Challenge helped Sylvester grow, where AI is
already changing drug discovery, and why a doctor's first responsibility is
to listen.

Watch the full conversation on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/ctFSn2-4hL4),
listen through the [podcast feed](https://share.transistor.fm/s/2b5debd5), or
explore the complete [episode page](https://betterness.ai/augmentedwellness/005-stephen-nimer).

## Build With The Episode

The **DCC Research Navigator** turns the public Faces of the DCC research list
into a navigable research-literacy tool. It helps riders, donors, patients,
and families:

- see the cancer areas and scientific themes represented by DCC-highlighted
  researchers;
- translate research language into better questions;
- explore the network in a browser or with an LLM;
- keep claims tied to the evidence actually available.
