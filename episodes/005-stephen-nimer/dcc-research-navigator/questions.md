# Questions Worth Asking

## For A Rider Or Donor

- What kinds of research can philanthropic funding accelerate before a larger
  traditional grant is available?
- How does a cancer center decide where flexible funding has the most
  leverage?
- Which areas in this map are prevention-focused, and which are treatment-
  focused?
- How long can the path be from a funded scientific question to something
  that changes patient care?
- What evidence should donors look for when a campaign describes impact?

## For A Patient Or Family

- Is this work about prevention, diagnosis, treatment, resistance, recovery,
  or quality of life?
- Is the research in a laboratory, an observational study, an early trial, or
  established clinical practice?
- What question could I take to my oncology team without assuming the
  research applies to me?
- What is known, what is promising, and what remains uncertain?

## For A Researcher

- Which bottleneck is the work trying to remove?
- What would count as a meaningful result?
- What must happen before the finding can be tested in patients?
- Where could AI accelerate the work, and where is human judgment essential?
- What claim about this field is commonly overstated?

## For Dr. Nimer

- What can DCC support make possible that would be harder through traditional
  funding alone?
- Which areas represented in this map are closest to clinical translation?
- How should a rider understand the path from a finish line to a research
  program?
- How did DCC support Sylvester's growth from a local powerhouse to an
  NCI-designated national destination?
- What should the public understand about "cures for cancer" in the plural?
- Where is AI already useful in cancer research, and what still requires a
  physician-scientist's judgment?
