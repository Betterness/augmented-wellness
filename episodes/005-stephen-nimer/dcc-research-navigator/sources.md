## Provenance Boundary

The graph is source-grounded but partly classified for navigation. Cancer
areas, themes, and possible impact categories help a non-specialist explore
the material. Those categories do not establish paper-level funding or
clinical causality.

External literature can be added later only when each record includes its
source and keeps "authored by or related to" separate from "funded by DCC."
