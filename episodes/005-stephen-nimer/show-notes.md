# Episode 005 - Dr. Stephen Nimer

Dr. Stephen Nimer has spent his career studying cancer, treating patients,
and building institutions capable of moving research into care. As director
of Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center, he is also helping shape Miami's
growth as a health and research hub.

The conversation covers the path that led him to leukemia research, what it
takes to lead a comprehensive cancer center, how cancer treatment is changing,
and why the future of care still begins with listening to the patient.

Dr. Nimer and Demian also return to the Dolphins Cancer Challenge, where they
first met as cyclists, and the community that has turned movement into support
for cancer research. The episode closes with a live Bett-i conversation and a
look at how public DCC research information can become easier for patients,
families, riders, and donors to explore.
