# 06 — Betterness Connection

This portfolio becomes more powerful when it is connected to your real data.

As Bett-i migrates into [betterness.ai](https://betterness.ai), the intended workflow is:

1. Create your Cycling + Recovery Context Portfolio.
2. Go to [betterness.ai](https://betterness.ai).
3. Create or connect your account.
4. Connect wearable and training sources when available.
5. Upload or connect labs if you want the system to understand recovery, performance, and health context together.
6. Ask Bett-i questions using your portfolio plus your permissioned data.

## What Bett-i can add

With permission, Bett-i can help connect:

- sleep trends;
- HRV and resting heart rate;
- training volume and intensity;
- ride files or summaries;
- labs and health markers;
- goals and constraints;
- prior conversations and corrections.

## Better questions once connected

- What changed in my recovery before my hardest week?
- Is my training load rising faster than my recovery context supports?
- What should I ask my coach after looking at the last 30 days?
- Which wearable signal is most consistent for me personally?
- What labs or health context might be relevant before I chase more volume?

## Boundary

Bett-i should help organize context and generate better questions. It should not diagnose, prescribe, or override a clinician or coach.
