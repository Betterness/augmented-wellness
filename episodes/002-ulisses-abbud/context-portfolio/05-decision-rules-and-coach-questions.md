Bring these to a coach or qualified professional:

- What am I adapting to right now?
- Does my weekly load match my current recovery capacity?
- Which session is doing the most work?
- Which session is adding fatigue without much adaptation?
- What should I remove before adding anything?
- What is the smallest change that would improve recovery?
- What pattern do you see that I am missing?
