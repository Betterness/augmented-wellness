# Episode 004 — Dr. Rabin Rahmani: Digestive Health Context Interviewer

Dr. Rabin Rahmani grew up during the Iran-Iraq War, came to the United States, and helped build one of Brooklyn's largest gastroenterology practices. The conversation moves from resilience and medical entrepreneurship to colon-cancer prevention, endoscopic innovation, the microbiome, GLP-1s, and the place AI may earn inside a clinical practice.

The episode's central idea is that digestive-health information becomes more useful when it is organized around the person: what changed, when it changed, what else was happening, what has already been tested, and what remains uncertain.

## Build the artifact

**[Create your own Digestive Health Context Interviewer](./artifact.md).** It is a Markdown-first interview system for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, a local agent, or another capable LLM. It asks one useful question at a time and turns scattered symptoms, food patterns, medications, tests, procedures, and goals into a living context portfolio and an appointment brief.

Start here:

1. Open [`digestive-health-context-interviewer.md`](./digestive-health-context-interviewer.md) in your AI tool of choice.
2. Let it interview you progressively rather than completing a long form at once.
3. Save the result in [`context-portfolio-template.md`](./context-portfolio-template.md).
4. Use [`questions/appointment-prep.md`](./questions/appointment-prep.md) to create a concise brief for a qualified clinician.
5. Connect [Betterness](https://betterness.ai) when you want permissioned reports, labs, wearables, goals, and daily context to enrich the portfolio.

The system is designed to improve the quality of context and questions. It does not diagnose, interpret a result as medical advice, or replace a gastroenterologist.
