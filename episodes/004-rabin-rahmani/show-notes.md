# Show notes

Dr. Rabin Rahmani joins Demian Bellumio for a conversation about resilience, medicine, entrepreneurship, gut health, and the practical role AI can play around a physician.

## In this episode

- Growing up in Iran during war and leaving as a child.
- Starting over in New York and choosing medicine.
- Why gastroenterology combines technology, procedures, and long-term patient care.
- The risk behind building Gastroenterology Associates of Brooklyn.
- Why physicians need AI to reduce non-clinical work and recover patient time.
- Medical education, social media, and the challenge of preserving a doctor's voice.
- Endoscopic innovation and AI-assisted polyp detection.
- Fatty liver, microbiome research, and individualized disease patterns.
- Why a laboratory range is not the same thing as clinical context.
- Protein, fiber, GLP-1s, IBS, IBD, and EPI.
- A live Bett-i agent search on slowly fermentable fiber, protein, and insulin sensitivity.
- Practical foundations: sleep, balanced nutrition, movement, screening, and appropriate medical care.
