# Create your own Digestive Health Context Interviewer

Digestive-health stories are usually scattered across memory, food logs, medications, procedures, lab reports, and separate appointments. A model that sees only one result can sound confident without understanding the person behind it.

This artifact starts differently. It interviews first.

## What it builds

The interviewer creates a reusable Markdown portfolio covering:

- the symptom timeline and what changed;
- food, bowel, sleep, stress, travel, and activity patterns;
- medications, supplements, allergies, and prior responses;
- family history, diagnoses, procedures, labs, and reports;
- current care team, goals, constraints, and unanswered questions;
- missing information that should be clarified rather than guessed.

It then produces a short appointment brief that separates known facts, reported patterns, open questions, and decisions that belong with a qualified clinician.

## Three ways to use it

1. **LLM project context** — add the Markdown files to Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, a local model, or another assistant so future conversations begin with the same organized context.
2. **MCP resource** — serve the folder through MCP so connected tools can read the portfolio without pasting it into every conversation.
3. **Betterness connection** — connect [Betterness](https://betterness.ai) and explicitly choose which reports, labs, wearables, goals, and daily signals may enrich the portfolio.

## Start

Open [`digestive-health-context-interviewer.md`](./digestive-health-context-interviewer.md), then save the interview into [`context-portfolio-template.md`](./context-portfolio-template.md).

The interviewer is not a diagnostic system. Its job is to make your history more complete, make uncertainty visible, and help you ask better questions.
