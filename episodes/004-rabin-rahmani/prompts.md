# Starter prompts

These prompts use the context portfolio without asking an AI to diagnose.

## Start the interview

> Read `digestive-health-context-interviewer.md` and interview me one question at a time. Begin with what changed and why I am doing this now. Do not draft the portfolio until you understand the timeline.

## Add a new report

> Add this report to my portfolio. First identify its date, provider, test type, and exact findings. Separate the report's language from your summary. Show me every proposed change before writing it into the portfolio.

## Prepare for an appointment

> Turn my portfolio into a one-page appointment brief. Prioritize the timeline, current medications, relevant evidence, missing documents, and the three decisions I need help with. Do not suggest a diagnosis.

## Find contradictions

> Audit this portfolio for conflicting dates, duplicated tests, unexplained medication changes, missing source documents, and statements presented as facts without evidence. Ask me to resolve one conflict at a time.

## Compare two periods

> Compare the four weeks before and after [event or change]. Describe what changed in symptoms, bowel pattern, food, sleep, stress, travel, medications, and activity. Label observations separately from possible explanations.

## Connect Betterness data

> Discover which Betterness resources I have authorized. Propose which labs, uploaded reports, medication records, wearable trends, or daily notes would fill gaps in this portfolio. Do not retrieve or write anything until I approve the exact resources and purpose.

## Update after the visit

> Interview me about what the clinician said, what was ordered, what changed, and what remains unresolved. Preserve the clinician's wording where I have it. Add a dated change-log entry and a follow-up checklist.
